# Task: browser-wikipedia

**App:** a web browser (Chrome or Safari), one window, nothing private open.

**Start state:** a new, empty browser tab.

**Steps, in order:**
1. Type `wikipedia.org` in the address bar and press Enter.
2. Click the search field and type `Large language model`, then press Enter.
3. If a search-results page appears instead of the article, click the "Large language model" result.
4. Scroll down to the "History" section, or click "History" in the table of contents.

**Success:** the "Large language model" article is open and its "History" section is on screen.

**What it exercises:** a navigation with a concrete URL (could become a script step), typed text, and a scroll or click on page content.
